# PERVOX brand identity

PERVOX combines PER, from the founder's surname Perovano, with VOX (Latin: voice). Brand concept: helping businesses transform ideas into effective technology.

Colors: Navy #101B2B, Steel #263B52, Gold #B38C54, Champagne #D4B284.

Assets:
- pervox-symbol.svg: geometric V symbol and central cube
- pervox-favicon.svg: navy square icon
- pervox-horizontal-dark.svg and pervox-horizontal-light.svg: branded horizontal lockups
- /assets/img/favicon.svg: replacement compatibility favicon for article pages

Both homepages use separate SVG icon plus live HTML text for readable mobile typography.

The legal entity remains L R PEROVANO LTDA; the existing email contato@lrperovano.com.br remains unchanged until a new mailbox is configured. Legacy PNG/ICO, Apple touch and social images need regeneration to complete the visual rebrand.
