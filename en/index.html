Digital Experiences & Corporate Websites

Corporate websites and landing pages that are fast, well structured for Google and designed to turn visits into inquiries.

A good fit when
  • your company has no website yet, or has one that doesn't inspire trust;
  • a new product or service needs a page to present and sell it;
  • your current website barely generates inquiries.

Custom Software Engineering

Web applications, dashboards, APIs and complete platforms — with login, customer area and payments — designed around the way your company works.

A good fit when
  • an important process depends on spreadsheets or manual controls;
  • your current system can't keep up with your business rules;
  • you need to offer data or services to partners through an API — the door through which systems exchange information.

Systems Integration & Automation

We connect the systems your company already uses so information flows without retyping, and we automate repetitive tasks that eat up your team's time.

A good fit when
  • the same information is typed into more than one system;
  • reports and reconciliations are put together by hand;
  • handing information between departments causes errors and rework.

Applied AI & Intelligent Workflows

Assistants, search across your company's documents and knowledge bases, and AI-powered automation — adopted when they are the right path for the problem.

A good fit when
  • your team spends time looking for answers in documents and records;
  • customer service or triage follows patterns an assistant can support;
  • there are too many texts or documents to analyze manually.

When a simpler solution does the job, that's what we recommend.

How we work

From diagnosis to evolution, with clarity at every step.

  1. 01

    Understanding the challenge

    We start by understanding your operation, the problem and what needs to change.

    What you get
    A shared understanding of the challenge and the priorities.
  2. 02

    Defining the solution

    We turn the diagnosis into a scope proposal: what will be built, in what order and why.

    What you get
    A scope proposal so you can decide with clarity.
  3. 03

    Development and validation

    We build in increments you can follow and test, adjusting course based on real use.

    What you get
    Working software, validated with the people who will use it.
  4. 04

    Evolution

    After delivery, the solution evolves within the agreed scope as your business changes.

    What you get
    Improvements and adjustments within what was agreed.

Timelines, follow-up format and terms are defined in each proposal, according to scope.

About

A close technical partner, from problem to solution.

PEROVANO is a software engineering company based in Brazil. We build websites, custom software, integrations, automation and artificial intelligence solutions for companies that need to create or evolve their digital products.

We work with judgment: we explain the problem, the approach and the expected benefit in language decision-makers understand — and we only recommend a technology when it fits the challenge. Projects are run remotely and can be conducted in English.
