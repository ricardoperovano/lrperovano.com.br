Clipe Forge Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Summary: Clipe Forge uses your TikTok account only to publish the videos you choose, with the caption and settings you set. We do not read your videos, followers or messages, we do not sell data and we use nothing for advertising. You can disconnect and delete everything at any time.

1. Who we are

Clipe Forge is a web app by PERVOX (L R PEROVANO LTDA; "we") that helps creators produce short vertical videos and publish them to the TikTok account the creator connects. Clipe Forge is not affiliated with, sponsored or endorsed by TikTok.

2. Data we receive from TikTok

When you connect your account with TikTok Login Kit and authorize access, we receive only:

  • Basic profile information (scope user.info.basic): account identifier (open_id), display name and avatar, to show which account is connected;
  • Access and refresh tokens, which allow sending videos on your behalf (scopes video.upload and video.publish);
  • Creator information returned by the API at posting time (nickname, allowed privacy options, maximum video duration and whether comments, duets and stitches are available), used only to build the posting screen;
  • The identifier and status of each post made by Clipe Forge, to show your sending history.
  • We do not access your other videos, followers, likes, comments or messages
  • We never post without an action from you
  • We do not sell, rent or share this data with advertisers

3. How we use the data

We use TikTok data only to: show the connected account; send to TikTok the videos you choose, as a draft in your inbox or as a direct post, with the caption, privacy level, interaction settings and disclosures you set on the posting screen; and show the status of those posts. Your TikTok account data is not sent to artificial intelligence providers.

4. Content you create and send

The videos, covers and captions you create in Clipe Forge are stored on our servers so you can review, schedule and send them. You can delete them at any time. Parts of the content (script, narration and images) may be generated with artificial intelligence tools at your request; for that reason, videos may be labelled as AI-generated content when published.

5. Storage and security

TikTok tokens are stored encrypted and are never sent to the browser. Traffic to the app and to TikTok uses HTTPS, and server access is restricted.

6. Retention, disconnection and deletion

  • You can disconnect your TikTok account in Clipe Forge at any time: we revoke the access with TikTok and delete the tokens.
  • You can also remove access from the TikTok app, in the security and app permission settings.
  • To delete all your data (account, videos and sending history), write to the e-mail below. We reply within 30 days.
  • We keep the sending history while your account exists.

7. Third-party services

Clipe Forge uses the TikTok API (subject to TikTok's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy) and infrastructure providers for hosting and file storage.

8. Minimum age

Clipe Forge is intended for people aged 18 or over. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

9. Your rights

Under the LGPD (Brazil) and the GDPR (Europe), you can request access, correction, portability or deletion of your data and withdraw consent at any time, through the e-mail below.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy; the date at the top shows the latest version. Relevant changes will be announced in the app.
